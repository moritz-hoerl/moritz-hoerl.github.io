I am a PhD student at the Vienna University of Economics and Business (WU), where I am affiliated with the [Economics of Inequality institute (INEQ)](https://www.wu.ac.at/ineq/home). My research focuses on social mobility, drawing on economics, statistics, sociology and philosophy. At its core is a simple question: how strongly does the broader family background shape children's outcomes?

My work combines empirical and conceptual perspectives. On the empirical side, I estimate intergenerational persistence and apply causal inference methods to better understand the mechanisms behind it. But I also like to take a step back and ask more fundamental questions: How do we and how should we measure social mobility? And what do ordinary people mean when they talk about it?
